How we approach qualitative research

Strong research starts before the questionnaire. It begins by defining the decision, the people who can answer it and the evidence needed to move with confidence.

01

Clarify the business or research decision before recommending a method.

02

Define the audience, market and feasibility considerations before launch.

03

Select research and fieldwork routes that fit the respondent group and location.

04

Build quality controls into screening, collection, monitoring and documentation.

05

Prepare outputs so findings can be reviewed by client teams and stakeholders.
